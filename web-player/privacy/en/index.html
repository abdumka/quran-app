Privacy Policy — Al-Mushaf Al-Jame

1.5.5 | Revision: 2026-10-22

This policy explains how Al-Mushaf Al-Jame (المصحف الجامع), Android package com.mahfodqr.qalon_mushaf, handles data in release 1.5.5. The app does not require an account. Contact for support and privacy requests: abdulrahman.keilani@gmail.com.

Microphone and recitation recognition

The app requests microphone access when you start voice-based Tasmee or memorization testing. Recitation recognition runs on your device using a downloaded speech model. Recognition does not require sending your voice to a cloud speech-recognition service. You can refuse microphone access and continue using features that do not need it, or revoke access in Android settings.

Information stored on your device

Settings, bookmarks, reading position, recitation reports, mistakes and memorization statistics are stored locally. Voice recitation sessions are also saved as audio files and logs containing recognition results, timing, page information, app and operating-system information, and a random installation identifier. The default retention is the most recent 10 sessions; you can change the number in the session-log settings. The local installation identifier is not a user name or account.

Sharing and uploads you choose

When you choose to share a recording or report, the selected files or information are handed to the app or recipient you select through the share interface. Files may include your voice, recitation content, test results, session details and installation identifier. Recipients handle their copies under their own policies; deleting the local copy does not delete theirs. In builds that offer a developer log-upload button, pressing it sends the selected files to the developer's private Cloudflare R2 storage. Sharing or uploading is separate from on-device recognition and does not occur automatically merely because you use the microphone.

Notifications and Firebase identifiers

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations to register an app installation and receive notifications. Google handles installation identifiers, push tokens and technical connection information needed to provide these services, including IP addresses. The app subscribes to general notification topics for its platform; the inspected code does not send the push token to a separate developer backend. Notification display permission is separate from service registration, so disabling displayed notifications does not necessarily stop Firebase network activity. Firebase push notifications may be unavailable on Huawei devices without Google Play services.

Downloads and third-party connections

The app makes network requests to download recitations, tafsir, speech models and related content, and to check for updates. Hosts include audio.mushaf-qaloon.com, quran-content.mushaf-qaloon.com, Cloudflare R2 and GitHub through raw.githubusercontent.com. These providers may receive your IP address, requested resource, request time and ordinary technical request information. A requested resource may reveal which content you choose to download or listen to. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. Opening external links or store pages, or emailing support, lets the chosen destination handle the information it receives under its own policies.

Storage and other permissions

Downloads and recordings normally use app storage. Bookmark backup export and import use file saving and selection. The Android manifest includes legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE declarations inherited from the file-saving component; write permission is limited to Android 9 and earlier. The app does not request all-files management access or scan your photo or document library. Notification permission supports announcements and reminders; boot access restores reminder schedules; vibration provides feedback; media foreground-service and wake-lock permissions support recitation playback.

Analytics and crash reporting

No Google Analytics for Firebase SDK, Firebase Crashlytics SDK, advertising SDK, user-account system, or location/contact permission request was found in the inspected release dependencies and code. This does not mean there are no network requests, Firebase identifiers or technical logs held by service providers.

Deletion, controls and retention

The recitation session-log screen lets you delete an individual session or all sessions and choose how many sessions to retain. Older sessions are automatically pruned when the retention limit is exceeded. You can manage bookmarks and downloads through the available app controls. To remove local app data, including settings and reports, use Android Settings, Apps, Al-Mushaf Al-Jame, Storage, Clear data. Independently exported files, copies held by sharing apps and system backups may need to be removed separately. Local deletion does not remove identifiers or logs held by Google or other providers; their policies apply. Server-log retention periods cannot be established from client code. For questions or deletion requests concerning information received by the developer, email abdulrahman.keilani@gmail.com with enough information to identify the files; do not send secrets or extra recordings unless needed.

Contact and changes

Contact for support and privacy requests: abdulrahman.keilani@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes; the page identifies its revision date.
