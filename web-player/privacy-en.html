Privacy Policy

المصحف الجامع (Al-Mushaf Al-Jami) app

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · العربية

What we collect

The app needs no account, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking. Nothing reaches us from the app unless you send us a message (see "Messages you send us" below).

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, settings, and Tasmee reports and recordings are stored only on your device. You can delete them in the app, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only for Tasmee (recitation check), and only with your permission. Audio is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Internet

  • The internet is used to download recitations and tafsir and to check for updates. None of your data is sent with these requests.
  • The app uses Google Firebase to deliver notifications. For this, Google creates a technical device identifier. It reaches us only if you send us a message, so we can notify you of our reply.

Messages you send us

If you use «راسلنا مباشرة» (message us directly):

  • Your message, any photo or PDF you attach, and (only if you tick the box) the app version and phone type are sent to us.
  • They are stored on our server at Cloudflare. Only our two support staff can read them.
  • Your notification identifier is stored with the conversation so we can tell you when we reply.
  • We never ask for your name, email or phone number. Photos are shrunk on your device and their location and camera details are removed before sending.
  • You can delete the whole conversation from the app at any time. We delete attachments when they are no longer needed.
